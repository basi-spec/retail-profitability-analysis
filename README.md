Retail Profitability & Revenue Leakage Analysis (Excel)
An advanced Excel project that finds out why a retail group's revenue rose 39% while its net profit fell 9%.
Business question
Revenue is growing, but profit is not. Where is the money leaking?
Key findings (Jan-Oct 2026 vs Jan-Oct 2024)
Revenue +39% ($2.36M to $3.27M); net profit -9% ($280K to $256K); profit per $1 sold 11.9c to 7.8c
Gross margin fell from 25.8% to 20.9%
Discount value rose 87% ($169K to $316K); sales above 10% discount earned only $17K of $684K gross profit
Electronics: 37% of revenue but 6% of gross profit; loss-making above 10% discount
Refunds up 96%; average product unit cost up about 8%
Six-month forecast about $2.45M revenue; discount discipline scenario lifts net margin from 7.8% to 11.1%
What is inside the workbook
Sheet	Purpose
Sales_Clean	Cleaned sales data (37,743 lines)
Excluded_Rows	12 invalid rows set aside
Products, Stores, Cost_History, Targets, Expenses, Returns	Supporting tables
Profit_Summary	Revenue to net profit by year
Pivot_* sheets	Year, region, product, discount band, seasonality, target and returns analysis
Monthly_Revenue, Forecast	Monthly trend and six-month forecast
Scenarios	Four what-if scenarios
Dashboard	Interactive executive dashboard with Year and Region filters
Tools and skills
Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot / Data Model, DAX, PivotTables, Forecast Sheet, What-If analysis, dashboard design.
Recommendations
Cap electronics discounts at 10%, reprice low-margin devices, pass cost increases into prices, reduce refunds, and track margin monthly.
Notes
The dataset is synthetic, generated with deliberate data-quality problems for cleaning practice.
All comparisons use the same January to October window in each year.
